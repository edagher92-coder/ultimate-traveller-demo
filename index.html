There’s no such thing
as a small question.

I don’t know exactly where I want to go. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Start with the feeling you’re after, who is travelling and a rough idea of timing. The proposed planning approach begins with a conversation; you don’t need a finished itinerary.

Can Susie help if I’m too busy to plan?

That is part of this personal planning approach: talk through your priorities, compare the possibilities and help bring the agreed arrangements together. The exact work and any fees would be confirmed with you before proceeding.

Is this only for first-time travellers?

No. You might want patient guidance for your first big trip, or a thoughtful partner for a more complex journey. Honeymoons, Europe, cruises, Africa and well-earned breaks are the travel interests explored here.

What happens after I share an idea?

In this browser-only demo, you receive a saved enquiry reference. Nothing is emailed, booked or charged. A published site would need tested enquiry delivery and Susie’s agreed follow-up process before launch.

Are the specials available to book?

The cards show demo offer states. A draft or scheduled idea is not an available offer. Supplier details, dates, price, inclusions and terms need verification and approval before any actual booking. Planning fees and any introductory promotion also need Susie’s confirmation.
